Lead management

A walk in or a call becomes a lead in ten seconds. Every lead has an owner and a next date, and the ones that go quiet are flagged before they are forgotten.

What the owner sees on this screen

  • See every lead across the team, with its owner and next date
  • Numbers at the top show new leads, calls due today and leads gone quiet
  • A team strip shows each person's open leads and who has the most going quiet
  • Nudge the owner of a quiet lead on WhatsApp, or hand the lead to someone else
  • Win or lose a lead with a reason, so you learn why sales are lost
Talk about this process

Try it: nudge the owner of a quiet lead, reassign one, or open a lead for its history. Example data only. Nothing here is saved.

How it helps you organise and save time

What changes for you and your team once lead management runs this way.

How it organises your work

  • Every enquiry from calls, walk ins, WhatsApp and ads lands in one list
  • Each lead has an owner, a stage and a next follow up date
  • Leads that go quiet turn red and reach the owner

How it saves time

  • No more asking who called whom, the list already says
  • Each person gets their call list every morning without being asked
  • Reasons for lost sales are recorded, so the same mistake is not repeated
