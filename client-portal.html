How it helps you organise and save time

What changes for you and your team once client portal runs this way.

How it organises your work

  • Each customer has a private page with only their own information
  • Every stage change updates it, so they are informed at every stage
  • Quotes, orders, documents, dues and questions sit in one place, and every action is shown to you

How it saves time

  • The portal replaces the WhatsApp updates you type by hand
  • Fewer where is my order calls, because the answer is already on the page
  • Documents arrive in the right place, and customers pay without being chased
