What decides where your price falls

Two businesses can ask for the same process and need very different builds.

A lead list for one salesperson is simple. A lead process with five departments, nine stages and a different follow up rule for every product is a different piece of work. Here is how we place you.

Simple
Starting price applies
  • One team or department
  • Up to 5 stages
  • One follow up rule
  • A handful of users
Typical
Starting price plus tailoring
  • Two or three teams
  • 6 to 8 stages
  • A few follow up rules by customer type
  • Your own fields, wording and a simple report
Complex
Priced after a short call
  • Four or more departments
  • Many stages or branches
  • Conditional follow ups and approvals
  • Several locations or user roles

Not sure where you fall? Tell me how many teams, stages and follow up rules you have today, and I will tell you which column you are in before you commit to anything.
