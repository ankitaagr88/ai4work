Renewals and daily report

Renewal dates are flagged well ahead, so nothing lapses. Every evening the owner gets one short message that says how the day went.

What the owner sees on this screen

  • Renewals sorted by days left, with the amount at stake
  • Remind the customer on WhatsApp, or mark it renewed in one tap
  • The evening report counts leads, work done and money collected
  • Choose what time the report arrives and who receives it
  • The report updates from what your team did today
